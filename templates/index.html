About This Tool

This tool applies behavioral economics frameworks to identify how pricing strategies exploit cognitive biases. Based on Kahneman & Tversky's prospect theory and research into behavioral pricing strategies.

Detected Biases

  • Anchoring: Initial price reference influences value perception
  • Sunk Cost Fallacy: Recurring costs create psychological investment
  • Loss Aversion: Fear of losing opportunities drives spending
  • FOMO & Scarcity: Limited availability creates perceived value
  • Endowment Effect: Bundled items feel more valuable than separate
  • Status Quo Bias: Default options attract more selections
  • Social Proof: Others' choices validate purchases
  • Decoy Pricing: Middle option makes expensive option better value
  • Dual Currency: Abstract currency obscures true spending
  • Artificial Scarcity: Time/quantity limits drive urgency

Based on behavioral economics research including Kahneman & Tversky's prospect theory, price discrimination strategies, and game monetization analysis.
